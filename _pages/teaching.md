Design della Mente I, Scuola Holden. Contemporary Humanities  
[FACULTY PROFILE](https://scuolaholden.it/albodocenti/nicola-zengiaro)

I teach Design della Mente at Scuola Holden, Italy’s leading school for contemporary humanities. My work focuses on post-anthropocentric storytelling, guiding students to explore and narrate the 'extended minds' of animals, plants, and minerals. By merging ecological psychology with creative practice, I help future storytellers deconstruct human-centric perspectives and develop a deeper, ecosystemic approach to narrative and design.
