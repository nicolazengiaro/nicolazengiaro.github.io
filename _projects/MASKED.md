I'm working for the Secondment in [MASKED](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101236781): Motor Health and Semiotic Function in the Kinesthetic Expressivity of Neurodegenerative Disease, funded under the MSCA Staff Exchanges scheme (HORIZON-MSCA-2024-SE-01-01), grant ID: 101236781 (2025-2029, total funding of 1.052.100 EUR).


</div>

In the MASKED project (MSCA Staff Exchanges), I undertake a research secondment in biosemiotic modeling to investigate the motor health and semiotic function underlying kinesthetic expressivity in neurodegenerative disease. Within Work Package 2 (Upskilling and Transfer of Knowledge), my role focuses on integrating and empirically applying biosemiotic approaches to the study of facial hypomimia in Parkinson’s disease..
