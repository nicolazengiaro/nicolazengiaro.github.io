I collaborate on the PRIN–PNRR project “[CULT-UP – Upcycling and Cultural Heritage](https://site.unibo.it/cultup/it/team-2)” financed by the European Union – NextGenerationEU, grant cod. P2022FZAEA (ERC: SH5_8)(2023–2025, total funding of €224.132), where I investigate how plastic waste affects animal interpretation processes and ecological communication.
